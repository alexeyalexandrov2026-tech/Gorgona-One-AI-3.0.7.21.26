## Photography

The photo pass includes 27 locally hosted licensed images: eight venue photographs from Wikimedia Commons, and nineteen free Unsplash editorial photographs marked as illustrative at the point of display. An editorial image never claims to show the named venue. Both card and detail view retain source/author and licence links; Creative Commons crops are indicated. Original asset hashes and provenance are recorded in `lib/dining/photos.mjs` and checked against the committed asset bytes.

Some actual venue photographs are archival; the implementation does not label them current photographs or imply a venue supplied them. Adding newer venue-issued media remains an editorial improvement, not a reason to remove photographic cards. Public availability of arbitrary venue or OpenTable pictures is not taken as a licence. No Unsplash+ assets or paid image subscriptions are used.
