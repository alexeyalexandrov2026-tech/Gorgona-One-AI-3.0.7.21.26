SMS alerts for the Miami Elio team

Miami Elio (gorgona-one.com) is a Miami luxury travel and ELIO+ Guide platform operated by MIAMI ELIO (Gorgona One AI), Miami, Florida, USA. This page describes the one text-message program we run: internal operational alerts sent to our own operator.

What these messages are

When something happens on the site that needs a human, such as a new trip request, a new ELIO+ Guide trip plan, or a partner or listing update, the system sends one short text message to the phone number of the business owner. Messages are sent only from our toll-free number, (833) 946-8811.

We do not send marketing, promotions or messages to customers or visitors. Visitors to the site never receive text messages from this program, and no visitor phone numbers are used for it.

Who consents, and how

The only recipient is the business owner. The owner opts in directly, in the following steps:

  1. The owner enters their own mobile number in the site's private server settings.
  2. The owner switches SMS alerts on in the private admin configuration. Alerts are off by default.
  3. The owner can switch them off in the same place at any time.

Consent statement: By turning on SMS alerts, the business owner agrees to receive operational text messages about activity on Miami Elio from (833) 946-8811. Message frequency varies with site activity and is capped at 50 messages per day and 500 per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, or HELP for help.

Sample message

Miami Elio: request created. Ref: 1a2b3c4d https://gorgona-one.com/admin Reply STOP to opt out.

Stop and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving them. Reply HELP for assistance, or write to support@gorgona-one.com.
